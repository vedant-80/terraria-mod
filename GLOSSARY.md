# NPC Conversations

Conversations and relationships between Terraria townsfolk and individual players.

## Language

**Personality**:
An NPC's enduring voice, disposition, interests and boundaries.

**Relationship**:
The familiarity and attitude an NPC has toward one player in one world.
_Avoid_: Sentience

**Affinity**:
The measure of an NPC's favorable or unfavorable attitude toward a player.

**Conversation memory**:
The limited recollection an NPC retains of its conversations with a particular player.

**Recent exchange**:
One player question and the NPC's reply.
